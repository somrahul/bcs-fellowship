Section 3A · Body of work — ResponsibilityStatement as submitted · 440 words
Situation

When I took over Emerging Technology, immersive computing wasn't a platform, it was five organizations running their own pilots. Every team had its own asset format and its own idea of finished. Nobody owned cross-platform performance. Features shipped without a name on the sign-off. That second one is the real problem. You can fix a slow renderer. You can't fix an organization where nobody is accountable for what ships.

Task

As Principal Product Manager for Emerging Technology I own five production AI products and the platform underneath them: View in Home: Paint, Beauty Virtual Try-On, Shop the Background, AI Audio and the generative 3D asset pipeline, built across five partner organizations. I hold production sign-off on all of it. That's the part that matters, because it's where shared accountability stops and one name goes on the release. That name is mine.

Action

Four things had to change, and I owned all four:

A portfolio, not a feature: I wrote the initiative plan and product requirements for each of them, and my name is on each as author in the product record. All five shipped. Shop the Background, start to finish: the one I'd hold up. I defined the product and wrote the requirements. I ran the launch as both Experiment Owner and Test Manager. I sequenced it so we could kill it cheaply, a fourteen-thousand item A/B first, scale only if customers bit. They bit. It now runs on iOS, Android and web across more than a hundred thousand item pages. Standards, and enforcing them: I set platform SLAs binding on every partner team, asset streaming under 2.0 seconds over cellular, 60 FPS in the viewport, automated regression on release. I've held launches when a budget was breached. A standard nobody enforces isn't a standard. I set the acceptance criteria an outside 3D vendor had to meet before we'd host their output. Settling the arguments: five organizations, five roadmaps. When inference latency, rendering fidelity and ship date pulled against each other, I made the call rather than escalating it.

Result

Those standards now govern immersive commerce across Walmart. View in Home: Paint lifted conversion 300 to 600 basis points, Beauty Virtual Try-On raised Gen Z engagement 10%, and the platform carries more than 165,000 AR-enabled items. These platforms generate over $100 million in annual enterprise revenue. Walmart's Make A Difference Award followed, and the company promoted me into the Principal grade in September 2026. The launch records and dashboards behind them are internal, and I'll walk an assessor through them on request. The number I'd point at isn't the revenue, it's the 165,000 items, because that's the one that says the platform holds.
