Section 3A: Responsibility

Experiential evidence demonstrating sustained accountability, technical leadership, and operational ownership over enterprise-scale AI, GenAI, and spatial computing platforms generating $100M+ in annual revenue.

Application Statement (STAR Technique • 381 Words)

Situation

In enterprise retail, deploying GenAI and spatial computing isn't just a software problem—it's an operational pipeline problem. When I took over immersive commerce at Walmart Global Tech, 3D visualization and augmented reality lived in disconnected pilots that couldn't scale across our multi-million SKU catalog or survive peak holiday traffic. The business needed interactive spatial experiences for 255 million weekly shoppers, but teams faced severe bottlenecks: manual 3D modeling took weeks per SKU at prohibitive cost, rendering latency degraded mobile conversion, and fragmented systems broke data handoffs.

Task

As Staff Product Manager for Emerging Technology, I was tasked with defining the product strategy, technical architecture, and execution roadmap for Walmart's enterprise immersive commerce ecosystem. My accountability spanned multimodal machine learning pipelines, generative 3D asset production, merchandising systems, and customer-facing experiences. I owned the platform standards, engineering quality gates, and live production sign-offs across web, native apps, and connected environments.

Action

I architected scalable data pipelines and enforced strict production contracts to industrialize immersive commerce:

  • Unified 3D Platform Strategy: I directed the multi-year roadmap across three asset generation pipelines—procedural geometry synthesis, diffusion-based GenAI modeling, and automated ingestion—establishing standardized 3D schemas, metadata validation, and automated quality gates.
  • Engineering Quality Gatekeeping: I led weekly technical reviews with engineering leads across five organizations, enforcing non-negotiable performance thresholds: sub-2.0s asset load over cellular networks and 60 FPS viewport rendering. I held releases whenever fidelity or frame-rate budgets were breached until architectural fixes were deployed.
  • Flagship Commercial Rollouts: I conceived and launched high-impact commercial platforms, including View in Home: Paint (combining AR visualization with real-time lighting simulation), Beauty and Optical Virtual Try-On, Shop the Background (applying Grounded DINO and SAM for real-time shoppable scene segmentation), and AI Audio.
Result

The platform architecture I established powers Walmart's global immersive ecosystem. We scaled the generative 3D asset catalog from 50,000 to over 200,000 production models, reducing unit production costs by 30% and turnaround time by 50%. This year, my platforms are generating over $100 million in revenue for Walmart, with combined GMV impact surpassing $140 million. View in Home: Paint delivered a 300–600 basis point conversion lift, and Beauty VTO drove a 10% engagement increase among Gen Z shoppers. My leadership provided end-to-end accountability, translating emerging AI into enterprise-grade commercial infrastructure serving millions of daily users.

Expanded Evidence Dossier & Deep Dives

1. Walmart Emerging Tech Platform Architecture

As Staff Product Manager for Emerging Tech at Walmart Global Tech (Sunnyvale, CA), Somesh Rahul directs technical strategy for Generative AI, Computer Vision, and Spatial Computing. He governs multi-quarter roadmaps across cross-functional engineering teams, computer vision data scientists, 3D artists, and merchant stakeholders.

  • Procedural 3D Pipeline: Automated procedural generation using Blender scripting and Substance Sampler textures informed by product catalog metadata.
  • GenAI Diffusion Generation: Automated reconstruction of 2D catalog imagery into 3D meshes using tailored Stable Diffusion models.
  • Automated Production Scale: Scaled asset ingestion from 50,000 to over 200,000 SKUs, driving a 50% reduction in turnaround time and 30% lower cost per asset.
  • Revenue Performance: Generating over $100M+ in annual revenue and driving $140M+ in combined GMV lift across web, app, and in-store channels.

  • Rendering Latency: Enforced sub-2-second asset load thresholds over 4G/5G connections.

  • Visual Fidelity Governance: Instituted automated mesh integrity and texture baking validation to eliminate visual artifacts in production.
