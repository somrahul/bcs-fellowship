Section 3C: Invention & Innovation

Evidentiary portfolio of 9 granted and published patents in IoT commissioning and intelligent load control, enterprise multimodal GenAI platforms, and book adoption at Rice University.

Application Statement (STAR Technique • 426 Words)

Situation

Two major computing domains in my career were constrained by severe scalability bottlenecks where conventional engineering approaches failed. In commercial building automation at Lutron Electronics, configuring wireless IoT load-control devices required labor-intensive, manual on-site commissioning with physical access to every node. In enterprise e-commerce at Walmart, generating photorealistic 3D assets relied on manual 3D artists, costing hundreds of dollars per SKU and taking weeks per asset. In ambient intelligence research, smart environments remained rigid and rule-based, lacking principled architectures for proactive intent inference, user privacy, and trust.

Task

I was responsible for inventing and productizing breakthrough technical architectures across all three domains: creating automated proximity-based wireless commissioning systems for distributed IoT networks, architecting an automated multimodal GenAI 3D generation engine at enterprise catalog scale, and formalizing foundational architectural blueprints for human-centered anticipatory AI systems.

Action

I invented and deployed novel algorithms, hardware-software protocols, and system frameworks that transformed industry standards:

  • Patented IoT Commissioning Architectures: I co-invented proximity-based beacon commissioning using RSSI-driven sorting logic, earning nine granted and published patents across the US, Europe, China, and Canada (including US 11,438,225 B2, US 12,166,627 B2, EP 3,935,791 B1, CA 3,115,114 C, CN 113170565 B, USD 940,160 S, USD 993,972 S1, USD 1,070,883 S1). This became the standard commissioning engine for Lutron Athena and residential systems (HomeWorks, RadioRA 3, Caseta), cutting installation labor by 20 to 30%.
  • Commercial Adoption & Citations: Acclivis Technologies (under CEO Rajeev Kaushal) integrated my patented method into standard commissioning libraries across non-lighting verticals (connected appliances, industrial IoT controllers), eliminating field pairing errors. The technology was licensed by ITS, integrated with Samsung SmartThings, cited by Apple, and adopted across 10 to 12 PassiveLogic patents.
  • Enterprise GenAI 3D Factory: At Walmart, I architected a multi-stage 3D asset engine combining procedural geometry generation, Substance texture synthesis, and diffusion-based GenAI models. I also developed Shop the Background, integrating Grounded DINO and SAM for real-time visual scene decomposition.
  • Published Book & Academic Adoption: I authored 'The Anticipatory Home: A Blueprint for AI, Spatial Computing, and Ambient Commerce', introducing the Living Services Architecture and Trust & Control Plane. Professor Vaibhav Unhelkar at Rice University formally evaluated and adopted these frameworks for graduate-level teaching in human-centered AI and autonomous agents.
Result

These inventions achieved widespread industrial and academic adoption. My IoT commissioning protocols became foundational standards across global commercial buildings, while Walmart's GenAI 3D factory scaled to over 200,000 production models, cutting turnaround time by 50% and asset costs by 30%. Concurrently, my book's frameworks at Rice University provide graduate researchers with concrete system-design patterns for next-generation anticipatory AI environments.

Expanded Evidence Dossier & Deep Dives

1. Patent Impact: Lutron Athena & Third-Party Enterprise Adoption (Acclivis)

Somesh's patented proximity-based commissioning architecture resolved a foundational industry bottleneck: how to pair and group identical wireless IoT devices in dense environments without manual serial number matching or physical label scanning.

  • Commercial Standard for Lutron: Powers Lutron's commercial flagship system (Athena) and residential portfolios (HomeWorks, RadioRA 3, Caseta), driving a 20–30% reduction in installation labor across commercial construction projects.
  • Enterprise Adoption by Acclivis Technologies: Rajeev Kaushal, CEO of Acclivis Technologies (former CEO of Benison Technologies & GM at eInfochips), documented that Acclivis adopted Somesh's patented RSSI proximity-ranking pattern across non-lighting verticals including connected appliances, embedded gateways, and industrial controller node grids:
    "We adopted Somesh’s patented pattern as a general commissioning approach in these projects... technicians can now simply stand near a device and confidently select the top entry. We saw clear reductions in commissioning time and configuration errors across multiple customer projects."
  • Independent Citations & Industry Standard: Cited by Apple in smart device discovery, adopted across 10–12 PassiveLogic patents for autonomous building controls, integrated with Samsung SmartThings, and licensed by Innovative Technology Solutions (ITS).
