Section 3D · Standing in the community — AwardsStatement as submitted · 390 words
Situation

Standing in this field isn't self-declared. It shows up when a professional body puts your record through review and moves you up a grade, or when a jury that has never met you gives your work an award. I've had both. Recognition on its own is a line on a CV. What makes it worth anything is whether it buys a seat where standards get set, and whether you use it.

Task

My aim is to turn recognition into service. Fellowship of a society, and the grade elevation that followed. International awards that put my team's work in front of juries with no stake in it. And the offices and committees that the standing has earned me a seat on.

Action

The record runs across three areas: Fellowship of the Soft Computing Research Society: SCRS elected me a Fellow on 22 October 2025, after independent review of my patents, products and publications. On 14 September 2026 they elevated me to Distinguished Fellow, certificate SCRS/DisFellow/1395. SCRS has more than 14,000 members. Fellows are about 5% of them, and Distinguished Fellows about a tenth of the Fellows. Both grades are conferred by review, not by application. International awards: Two Gold MUSE Design Awards in 2026, for Shop the Background and Dynamic Showroom. Two iF Design Awards in 2024, for Beauty Virtual Try-On and View in Your Home. The IC BAM Under 40 Award, 2026. Runner-up for Most Innovative AI Presentation at the 2025 IEEE New Era AI Summit. Internally, Walmart's Make A Difference Award and Lutron's GLF Innovation Award. Office and governance: I'm Treasurer of the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society's Dallas chapter. I sit on the Industry Advisory Committee for IEEE WCCST 2026 and the Advisory Board for the University of North Dakota CX Program. I delivered the CSCT 2025 keynote on ambient computing, and I served on the planning committee for World IA Day 2014 in Ann Arbor, where this started.

Result

Every one of these is external, none of it rests on my job title. The fellowship came from a society that reviewed my record, the awards came from juries with no stake in my career. The MUSE and iF records are public and searchable by year and category, so none of this rests on my word either. And the fellowship changed what I get asked to do: the WCCST committee seat, the CSCT keynote and the chapter office all came after it, not before. That's the test I care about. Not that the work was recognised, but that the recognition put me somewhere I could be useful to other people.
