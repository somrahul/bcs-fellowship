Section 3D: Standing in the Community

International design and technology awards, senior society fellowships, conference advisory appointments, keynotes, and peer review service.

Application Statement (STAR Technique • 385 Words)

Situation

In advanced computing and artificial intelligence, standing in the community is substantiated when an innovator's contributions earn competitive international awards, formal elevations from premier professional societies, and invitations to govern research conferences and peer-reviewed journals. My technical contributions across enterprise AI, computer vision, and IoT automation have achieved sustained recognition from global engineering bodies, and I prioritize channeling this standing back into advancing the computing discipline.

Task

My objective is to leverage my independent standing to elevate technical standards across the computing profession across three dimensions: competing for and winning rigorous international design and technology awards, evaluating candidates for senior professional grade elevations, and governing academic conferences and peer-reviewed literature.

Action

I maintain an active record of international awards, governance appointments, and scholarly review service:

  • Prestigious International Awards & Design Honors: My commercial AI and spatial computing systems have earned top global accolades, including two Gold MUSE Design Awards (2026) for Walmart Shop the Background and Dynamic Showroom; two iF Design Awards (2024) for Beauty Virtual Try-On and View in Home; the IC BAM Under 40 Award (2026) from the Indian Brand Convention; and the Most Innovative AI Presentation Award (Runner-up) at the 2025 IEEE New Era AI World Leaders Summit. Internally, I received Walmart's highest honor, the Make A Difference Award (top 1% performance rating), and Lutron's GLF Technology Innovation Award for myRoom VUE.
  • Senior Grade Elevations & Society Fellowships: I was elevated to IEEE Senior Member following independent peer evaluation of my decade-long engineering contributions. Furthermore, I was inducted as a Distinguished Fellow of the Soft Computing Research Society (SCRS), where I actively serve on the Steering Committee evaluating senior nominations for fellow grade elevation.
  • Conference Governance, Keynotes & Editorial Service: I serve on the Industry Advisory Committee for IEEE WCCST 2026 and the Advisory Board for the University of North Dakota CX Program. I delivered the Keynote Address at CSCT 2025 and regularly review scholarly manuscripts for Elsevier Data in Brief, Elsevier MethodsX, IEEE conferences, and CRC Press (Taylor & Francis).
Result

These awards and appointments validate that my technical work is recognized by international peers as setting industry benchmarks. By reviewing dozens of research papers, evaluating senior fellowship nominations, and speaking at global summits, I actively bridge the gap between enterprise AI productization and academic computing standards.
